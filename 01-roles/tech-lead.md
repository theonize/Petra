# Tech Lead

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Director  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-13

## Purpose

Ensure production systems and tech volunteers reliably serve the service with calm excellence — including at **group rehearsal**, not only Sunday.

## Owns

- Tech SOPs in `05-tech/` and equipment routines in `09-equipment/`
- Recruiting, training, and scheduling of tech roles (with Director)
- Input lists, stage plots, and show-file standards
- Escalation for broken gear and safety hazards
- Coordination with facilities / IT when needed
- Post-service strike standards
- Enforcing **tech prior-rehearsal attendance** and rehearsal-ready house + confidence

## Weekly rhythm

- Review roster coverage (FOH, monitors, media, lights, stream) for **rehearsal and service**
- Scan set for unusual needs (choir mics, guest artist, playback)
- Confirm show files / lyric decks are staged for midweek
- Capture issues in a running punch list

## Expectations for tech teammates

- Scheduled tech attends the **prior group rehearsal** (default: no rehearsal → do not serve)
- Media runs **overhead and confidence** during practice
- Exceptions only when granted with a qualified cover

## Success looks like

Call times staffed; midweek production path already works; fewer mid-service fires; volunteers know the checklist without heroics.
