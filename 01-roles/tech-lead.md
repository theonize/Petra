# Tech Lead

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Purpose

Ensure production systems and tech volunteers reliably serve the service with calm excellence.

## Owns

- Tech SOPs in `05-tech/` and equipment routines in `09-equipment/`
- Recruiting, training, and scheduling of tech roles (with Worship Lead)
- Input lists, stage plots, and show-file standards
- Escalation for broken gear and safety hazards
- Coordination with facilities / IT when needed
- Post-service strike standards

## Weekly rhythm

- Review roster coverage (FOH, monitors, media, lights, stream)
- Scan set for unusual needs (choir mics, guest artist, playback)
- Confirm show files / lyric decks are staged
- Capture issues in a running punch list

## Success looks like

Call times staffed; fewer mid-service fires; volunteers know the checklist without heroics.
