# Lighting Operator

**Owner:** Tech Lead  

## Purpose

Support focus, mood, and congregation comfort without turning the platform into a concert that excludes the room.

## Owns

- Cue list aligned to order of service
- Safe levels (no hazardous strobes without clearance)
- Stage wash sufficient for cameras and real faces

## Attendance

- **Prior group rehearsal required** when scheduled for that service — see [`../03-rehearsal/rehearsal-standards.md`](../03-rehearsal/rehearsal-standards.md)
- Prefer running key cues against the set at midweek when the weekend is cue-heavy

## Defaults

- Bright enough for people to feel invited to sing
- Dynamic for full-band moments; calmer for prayer/word
- Avoid cue spam that distracts from lyric

## Pre-service

- [ ] Patch / universe OK
- [ ] Cue stack matches plan
- [ ] Manual bump ready for unplanned moments
