# 01 — Roles

Role charters define **ownership**, not every micro-task. Detailed SOPs live in other folders.

| Role | In one line |
|---|---|
| [Director](director.md) | Owns the ministry under the pastor; appoints the team leads; accountable for the worship experience |
| [Worship Lead](worship-lead.md) | Owns **how** it is played — keys, forms, charts, rehearsal |
| [Song Selector](song-selector.md) | Chooses **what** we sing and why; hands the set to the Worship Lead |
| [Vocalist](vocalist.md) | Leads congregational melody with blend and presence |
| [Band musician](band-musician.md) | Rhythmic and harmonic foundation that serves the song |
| [Tech Lead](tech-lead.md) | Owns production standards and tech roster health |
| [FOH Engineer](foh-engineer.md) | The congregation’s mix — lead vocal and lyric first |
| [Monitor Engineer](monitor-engineer.md) | What the stage hears, without wrecking stage volume |
| [Media / Lyrics](media-operator.md) | Right words on the right screens at the right time |
| [Lighting](lighting-operator.md) | Focus and mood without distraction |
| [Stream / Broadcast](stream-operator.md) | A stable, clear online service |
| [Stage / Logistics](stage-logistics.md) | A safe, ready platform — plots, cables, spares |

**Notes:**

- One person may hold multiple roles on a given week (e.g. Song Selector + Worship Lead). When that happens, they still own each charter’s must-dos — or explicitly hand off pieces in the plan.
- **Service Planner** (order of service beyond the musical set) is a hat, not a separate charter — usually worn by the Director or Worship Lead. See [Definitions](../00-foundation/definitions.md).
