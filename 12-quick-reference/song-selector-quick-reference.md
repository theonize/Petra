# Song Selector Quick Reference

**Owner:** Director

Two pages for the person who decides **what** we sing and **why**. Every line points into the skeleton; if this sheet and the linked page disagree, the linked page wins. Clocks: [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md).

## The job in three lines

- You curate **what** we sing and **why** — theologically sound, pastorally timed, musically reachable for *this* room ([Song Selector](../01-roles/song-selector.md)).
- The Worship Lead owns **how** — keys, forms, charts, rehearsal — published by the keys/charts deadline ([Worship Lead](../01-roles/worship-lead.md)).
- You **propose**; the Director **confirms** — silence by the confirm-by time counts as confirmed. After that only a named exception changes titles ([Scope & Authority](../00-foundation/scope-and-authority.md)).

Success: the room recognizes enough of the diet to sing boldly; new songs are taught on purpose; Worship Lead and Media are not guessing titles the night before rehearsal. If you also wear the Worship Lead or Director hat, complete both charters.

## Weekly rhythm

1. Pull series notes, calendar, pastoral themes
2. Draft the set (or a short menu) with an intentional arc — opener energy, response moment, closer
3. Review with the Director (pastor when a lyric is sensitive)
4. **Publish the proposed set by the weekly deadline**; it is fixed on confirmation (or silence past the confirm-by time); hand off to the Worship Lead for keys, forms, charts, tracks
5. Confirm Media has titles early enough to build the deck; flag anything new, track-heavy, or production-special to Worship Lead / Media / Tech Lead
6. After Sunday: keep, rework the teach, or rest — one line in the register

Rehearsal may amend keys, forms, arrangements, and cues; wholesale song replacement is an exception you or the Director lead, not a rehearsal habit ([Rehearsal Standards](../03-rehearsal/rehearsal-standards.md)).

## Must before the Worship Lead locks rehearsal materials

- [ ] Proposed set published by the deadline and confirmed (or silence past the confirm-by time)
- [ ] Every title passes the policy and is in the rolling repertoire register
- [ ] At least one carryover from last week — or the Director exception is noted with a reason
- [ ] New-to-us songs flagged with teach intent; landmines called out (range, complex form, sensitive lyric)
- [ ] Rough flow notes if no full planner doc

Use the [Setlist Worksheet](../10-templates/setlist-worksheet.md); it carries the policy checklist and the repertoire count.

## Six core criteria — every song passes all six

| # | Criterion | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Biblically defensible theology | Faithful to Scripture in context; nothing the church must excuse or privately reinterpret? |
| 2 | Corporate-worship intent | Made for the gathered church to sing together, not to hear performed? |
| 3 | Congregational ownership | Can the gathered body truthfully say these words together? |
| 4 | Congregational singability | Can an untrained voice on row 12 sing it in the published key — melody, rhythm, range, repetition, phrasing? |
| 5 | Clear Christian confession | Unmistakably the God and gospel of the Christian faith — not generic transcendence or romance? |
| 6 | Pastoral usefulness | What work of worship and formation does it help this congregation do now? |

Plus: repertoire fit (adds a function, not a duplicate) and team stewardship (this week’s roster can deliver it). Source: [Song Selection Policy](../06-music-library/song-selection-policy.md).

## What we avoid, and the rules around it

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **50-title rolling ceiling** | No more than 50 distinct titles **sung in the trailing 12 months** — seasonal titles count; Rest/Archive titles count until they age out; “resting” a song does not free a slot today. A maximum, not a target |
| **We avoid secular / generic love songs** | In the worship set — a lyric that is only Christian once the leader explains it. Walk-in/transition music is a separate programming call |
| **We avoid singing in God’s voice** | The room singing continuously *as* God (“I, the Lord, say…”). Sung Scripture with a clear speaker cue is a leadership call |
| **Week-to-week continuity** | Carry at least one song from the preceding service; meaningful repeat over token repeat; Director may waive — record why |
| **Theology-sensitive lyrics** | Go to pastoral oversight before use |
| **Exceptions** | Director may grant a one-service exception to the defaults. The two avoidances are not the Director’s alone: **Director with pastoral oversight** may make a documented exception. Record every exception on the worksheet; if it recurs, revise the policy openly |

Add your church’s own “we allow / we avoid” rows in Our Congregation so the gray areas are owned locally.

## Repertoire-shaping defaults

1. **One unfamiliar song at a time** per service
2. **Teach through repetition** — plan the next uses before the first; ~3 outings in 6–8 weeks
3. **Known songs carry new songs** — most of each set is already owned by the room
4. **Prefer durable** over newly released or currently popular
5. **Cover the whole Christian vocabulary** across the year — adoration, creation, incarnation, cross, resurrection and reign, Spirit, confession, assurance, thanksgiving, lament, petition, mission, communion, baptism, hope
6. **Balance pronouns** — teach the church to sing *we*, address one another, tell what God has done
7. **Let text govern treatment**; **rest songs on purpose**; **evaluate fruit, not applause**
8. **Licensing and lyric accuracy** still apply ([Licensing](../06-music-library/licensing-ccli.md))

## New-song gate (before a title enters Trial)

Record in the [Annual Repertoire Register](../10-templates/annual-song-repertoire.md):

- Six criteria confirmed in writing · line-by-line lyric review with Scripture references and concerns
- Fits under the 50-title ceiling · named function the repertoire lacks
- Congregation-friendly key and teach strategy agreed with the Worship Lead · second and third outings scheduled
- In the licensed catalog (or rights cleared) · Director approval; pastoral review if theology-sensitive

## Lifecycle & review rhythm

**Scout → Trial → Core → Rest → Archive** ([Song Lifecycle](../06-music-library/song-lifecycle.md)). You own what enters and which titles this week; Worship Lead owns arrangement/rehearsal; Media/Tech Lead owns support; keep/rework/rest is a joint call after 2–3 outings.

| Cadence | Do |
|---|---|
| Per service | Carryover present or exception recorded; each title’s stage known; every title in the register |
| Quarterly | Count titles sung in the trailing 12 months; review participation, over-repetition, neglected themes, range, season; move titles deliberately |
| Annually | Full repertoire review with pastoral oversight; doctrinal alignment and gaps in sung theology; publish next year’s core list with room under the ceiling |

## Handoffs

**Director** — proposed set, exceptions with reasons, register count · **Worship Lead** — confirmed titles, teach intent, landmines · **Service Planner** — where songs sit · **Media** — titles and forms early · **Tech Lead** — choir, orchestra, tracks, guests.
