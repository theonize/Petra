## Why we are here

> **Encourage the congregation to sing.** ([Mission & Vision](../00-foundation/mission-vision.md))

The room’s voice is the success signal, not the platform’s. Attention goes **past** you to God — if people are watching the band, simplify, turn down, re-center the lyric ([Values](../00-foundation/values.md)). Petra settles the mundane so you are free to lead.

## The week, from your seat

| When | What happens | You |
|---|---|---|
| Set confirmed | Song Selector proposes, Director confirms; Worship Lead publishes keys, tempos, forms, charts by the charts deadline | Open the plan; flag range or capo trouble **now**; acknowledge chart/key changes |
| Before rehearsal | — | Practice to chart + reference (+ click if used); patches/scenes labeled; backup chart stowed |
| Midweek rehearsal (90 min min.) | Review previous service → warm-up → work every part and every **transition** → full run; house + confidence live | **Ready to rehearse** at the clock; specific mix requests; note your fixes |
| Sunday pre-service | Line check, confidence glance, 1–2 problem spots, opener + one transition, prayer | Present → ready for run-through → service-ready; **freeze** (default: doors) ends experiments |
| Service | Worship Lead, as **Platform lead**, makes musical calls and listens to the pastor | Watch the Platform lead; worship when not playing; reset with peace if something breaks |
| Strike + notes | People first, then process | Gear stowed; room not trashed; notes received without defensiveness |

## Before you walk into rehearsal

**Band** — chart learned (on confidence, or backup stowed); patches/scenes per song; capo and instrument changes planned; know motif vs pad vs groove per section; gear working, backups for critical items; tablet charged. Drummers: lock tempo/feel with the Worship Lead and any tracks before debating fills.

**Vocals** — reference heard in the **published** key; melody and assigned part learned; breaths, unison/harmony, descant marked; ad-libs only where planned; wardrobe per [guidelines](../08-culture-care/wardrobe-guidelines.md); warmed up enough to be useful.

**Both** — if something is not ready, tell the Worship Lead **before** the ready-to-rehearse time so the plan can be simplified.

## In the room

- Talk between songs, not over the Worship Lead; tune quietly; fix buzzes without a TED talk; phones for emergencies only
- Mix requests are specific (“more acoustic in the verse”) through the cue workflow — no shouting mid-song unless safety; **monitor freeze** after the final run
- Tell Media / Worship Lead about wrong or unreadable confidence content **at rehearsal**, not during the opener
- Test whether the **room** can sing it — an untrained voice on row 12, in the published key. Say so if not

## On the platform

- Invite the room to sing — volume, eyes, energy make space for their voice; be present, don’t perform emotion you aren’t carrying
- Mics are always hot; hand off intentionally; no venting on a live mic
- Hold still through prayer/scripture unless directed; tune under pads/prayer
- Lose a line? Mute or pull back — don’t invent loudly. Pastor extends? Watch the Platform lead — don’t invent a concert
- Offstage still represents the team (green room, lobby, chat); impaired serving is an immediate stop ([Platform Etiquette](../04-service/platform-etiquette.md))

## When something breaks

People safety first; preserve the moment second; one message on the day-of channel. Someone collapses → any lead stops the music. Click dies → Worship Lead counts in, live arrangement. Lyrics freeze → lead vocal carries, Media goes to a safe slide ([Incidents & Contingencies](../04-service/incidents-and-contingencies.md)).

## Scheduling & communication

| Situation | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Schedule request | Accept/decline within **48 h**; availability covers rehearsal **and** service |
| Swap | You find a **qualified** cover (on the roster for the role, or signed off); they take rehearsal *and* service; propose in the tool; final when both people **and** the swap confirmer agree; <24 h → phone the Worship Lead / Tech Lead / Director |
| Late / absent | Tell the Worship Lead or Director ASAP — before rehearsal starts when humanly possible |
| Sick / contagious | Stay home; a thin band beats a sick room |
| Key / chart change | Acknowledge within 12 h or before the next rehearsal |

([Communication Norms](../02-scheduling/communication-norms.md) · [Availability & Swaps](../02-scheduling/availability-and-swaps.md))

## Who to ask

Section lead → **Worship Lead** (musical) or Tech Lead (production) → Director → pastor. Care and safety issues skip the chain — designated reporter / pastor the same day ([Safety & Protection](../08-culture-care/safety-and-protection.md)).
