# Our Congregation

**Owner:** Director  

Petra ships generic. This page is the **one place** to write down what is true for **your** church — names, clocks, tools, rules — and points to the page where each value is used. Copy it, fill it in, keep it with your team's planning tool; the public runbook stays blank. Policy pages do not carry a second copy of these values.

## Two lists

**Petra defaults — already decided; change them in the open (Changelog), not by drifting**

- Encourage the congregation to sing — the test of every Sunday
- No prior rehearsal → no serve (default); exceptions are granted, not assumed
- Rehearse the way we serve: house lyrics + confidence monitors at midweek
- Confidence monitors, not music stands
- Proposed set by the weekly deadline; fixed on Director confirmation (silence = confirmed); keys/charts by the charts deadline
- Six song criteria; we avoid secular / generic love songs and singing in God’s voice; 50-title rolling ceiling; one new song at a time; carry one song week to week
- People safety first; protection policy outranks worship preference; “must” only for safety, care, or congregational clarity

**You must fill in before your first Sunday** — the tables below. A blank is a leadership decision still to make, not something a volunteer should guess.

## Before your first Sunday (adoption checklist)

- [ ] Name the pastor (or delegate), Director, Worship Lead, Song Selector, Tech Lead — and tell the team
- [ ] Give every teammate the church protection policy and the **designated reporter’s** name/number; confirm background-check / training requirements (blocking if minors serve)
- [ ] Link the emergency / evacuation plan; know AED, first aid, exits; name the day-of urgent channel
- [ ] Record tools (schedule, plan, chat, charts, files) in [Tools & Systems](../11-reference/tools-and-systems.md); credentials stay out of the repo
- [ ] Set the weekly clock (below); put service-specific exceptions in that week’s plan
- [ ] Decide which roles are combined locally and who decides when the usual owner is absent
- [ ] Confirm the rehearsal-attendance rule and its exception path with pastoral leadership; explain it at onboarding before anyone accepts a schedule
- [ ] Record licensing coverage and the reporting owner (below)
- [ ] Walk the runbooks in the building with one experienced leader and one newer volunteer; replace local jargon; check every instruction can actually be followed
- [ ] Give every teammate: the [Playbook](../PLAYBOOK.md), their [role charter](../01-roles/README.md) and [handout](../12-quick-reference/README.md), the plan link, the weekly clock, and the absence / safety / care contacts
- [ ] 30 days in: ask volunteers what was unclear, missing, or unrealistic; re-check after any incident, gear or leadership change, or church-policy revision
