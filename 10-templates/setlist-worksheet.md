## Selection-policy check

- [ ] Every song contains biblically defensible theology
- [ ] Every song is intended for corporate worship — meant to sing together
- [ ] The gathered body can truthfully own every lyric
- [ ] Melody, rhythm, range, repetition, and phrasing are congregationally singable (untrained voice, row 12, published key)
- [ ] Every song makes a clear Christian confession and has a pastoral use in this gathering
- [ ] No secular or generic “love song” — or a documented leadership exception below
- [ ] The congregation does not sing in God's first-person voice — or a documented leadership exception below
- [ ] No more than one unfamiliar song in this service
- [ ] New title has planned repeat outings, is in the licensed catalog, and appears in the annual register
- [ ] Theology-sensitive line or reading sent to pastoral oversight (if any)
