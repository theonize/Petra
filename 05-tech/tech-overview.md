# Tech Overview

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Tech Lead  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Philosophy

Technology is successful when nobody talks about it afterward — except to say they could hear and see clearly.

## Priorities

1. Safety (power, rigging, hearing, trip hazards)
2. Intelligibility (spoken word + lead vocal)
3. Stability (show files, backups, spares)
4. Beauty (mix, light, visual craft)
5. Experimentation (never at the cost of 1–3 on Sunday)

## Staffing minimums (edit to your reality)

| Service type | Minimum tech set |
|---|---|
| Normal weekend | FOH + Media (+ Monitors if not FOH-combined) |
| Full production | FOH, Monitors, Media, Lights, Stream, Stage |
| Simple / stripped | Combined roles OK if cross-trained |

## Hearing & levels

- Offer hearing protection for musicians/tech as needed
- FOH targets comfort for the congregation, not playlist loudness wars
- Document any house-curve or limiter policy church leadership affirms
