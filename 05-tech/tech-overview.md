# Tech Overview

**Owner:** Tech Lead  

## Philosophy

Technology is successful when nobody talks about it afterward — except to say they could hear and see clearly enough to **sing**.

Tech exists to clear friction for the platform and the room (same reason Petra exists): settle the mundane so presence is possible.

## Priorities

1. Safety (power, rigging, hearing, trip hazards)
2. Intelligibility (spoken word + lead vocal)
3. Stability (show files, backups, spares)
4. Beauty (mix, light, visual craft)
5. Experimentation (never at the cost of 1–3 on Sunday)

## Minimum viable Sunday (plain language, before the expert checklists)

| If you are on… | Sunday is OK when |
|---|---|
| **FOH** | The pastor’s mic works; the lead vocal is clear at the back; nothing hurts; you stopped changing things at freeze |
| **Monitors** | Everyone hears click (if used), lead vocal, and themselves; no one shouts for a mix mid-song |
| **Media** | The right words are on the house screens on time; confidence monitors show lyrics + section cues; you know the safe slide |
| **Lights** | People are visible (and on camera); no strobes; prayer and word are calm; you know blackout and restore |
| **Stream** | Audio is healthy and the stream is live at the agreed time; you know the fallback |
| **Stage** | Nothing to trip on; the spare mic, cable, and DI are where the plot says; inputs match the list |

## One tech, several hats — boot order

Most churches have one person wearing FOH + monitors + media. Do these **in order**; don’t start the next until the previous is true:

1. **Power** in the documented order (amps / powered speakers last)
2. **Show file / deck** loaded — the golden file, this week’s plan
3. **Wireless**: scan, fresh batteries, packs labeled
4. **Line check** every input; pastor mic first
5. **House + confidence up** and routed correctly (confidence content never on house)
6. **Spare vocal path** live (handheld on a known channel)
7. **Freeze** at doors — stop touching things

Write your room’s actual order in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md).

## Staffing minimums (edit to your reality)

| Service type | Minimum tech set |
|---|---|
| Normal weekend | FOH + Media (+ Monitors if not FOH-combined) |
| Full production | FOH, Monitors, Media, Lights, Stream, Stage |
| Simple / stripped | Combined roles OK if cross-trained — use the boot order above |

## Hearing & levels

Hearing is people-safety, not taste.

- Hearing protection is available at a known place (Our Congregation); offer it to musicians and tech
- If anyone — on the platform or in the room — reports pain, **turn down first**, then tell the Tech Lead
- FOH targets comfort for the congregation, not playlist loudness wars
- Record the house-level / limiter policy church leadership affirms in Our Congregation
