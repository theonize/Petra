# Local Adoption Checklist

**Owner:** Director + Pastoral oversight

## Purpose

Petra is a reusable framework, not a substitute for a church's leadership, safety policy, calendar, or production plan. Complete this checklist before calling the runbook ready for routine use. A blank is a leadership decision still to make—not a decision a volunteer should have to guess.

## Required before launch

- [ ] Name the Pastor/elder liaison, Director, Worship Lead, Song Selector, and Tech Lead. Tell the team who currently holds each role.
- [ ] Link the church's current child-protection, medical-emergency, evacuation, security, and incident-reporting policies. Confirm which official contact or ministry receives urgent reports.
- [ ] Record the official schedule, service plan, team communication, music-library, and file-storage systems in [Tools & Systems](tools-and-systems.md). Keep credentials and private pastoral information out of this repository.
- [ ] Set the normal publication deadline, rehearsal day, call times, ready times, service time, and freeze time. Put service-specific exceptions in that week's plan.
- [ ] Decide which roles may be combined locally and who makes a decision when the usual owner is absent.
- [ ] Confirm the rehearsal-attendance rule and its exception path with pastoral leadership; explain it during onboarding before asking volunteers to accept a schedule.
- [ ] Confirm local requirements for membership, minors, background checks, accessibility, and safeguarding with the people who own those policies.
- [ ] Record the canonical chart library, licensing/reporting owner, license coverage, and stream/recording practices. Seek qualified legal guidance where the church is unsure; Petra is not legal advice.
- [ ] Document the actual audio, monitor, lyric, lighting, stream, power-up, shutdown, and backup paths used in each room.
- [ ] Walk the runbooks in the building with both an experienced leader and a newer volunteer; replace unexplained local jargon and verify that every instruction can actually be followed.

## Publish to the team

Before launch, give every teammate:

1. the [one-page playbook](../PLAYBOOK.md);
2. their [role charter](../01-roles/README.md) and [quick-reference handout](../12-quick-reference/README.md);
3. the official schedule/service-plan link;
4. the normal rehearsal and service clock; and
5. the official absence, safety, and pastoral-care contacts.

Do not publish personal phone numbers, credentials, information about minors, or pastoral-care notes in a public copy of Petra. Use the church's approved private system.

## Review after launch

- [ ] Run a 30-day review with volunteers: what was unclear, missing, or unrealistic?
- [ ] Recheck the runbook after a material incident, facility or equipment change, leadership change, or church-policy revision.
- [ ] Record durable runbook changes in the [Changelog](../CHANGELOG.md); keep week-specific notes in the service plan instead.
