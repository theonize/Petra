# Platform Etiquette

**Owner:** Director  

## Presence

- **Invite the room to sing** — volume, eyes, and energy make space for their voice (see [Mission & Vision](../00-foundation/mission-vision.md))
- Engage the room; don’t perform *at* it (see [Definitions](../00-foundation/definitions.md) on worship vs performance language)
- **Point attention past yourself** — the set may pull people out of the world’s noise, but never away from God; if the room is watching you, make space and re-center the lyric
- When not playing, still worship — not disengaged gear fussing
- Watch the **Platform lead** for entries, holds, and endings
- Eyes up: use **confidence monitors** (video monitors); avoid stand clutter ([Lyrics & Stage Displays](../05-tech/lyrics-and-stage-displays.md))
- Water bottles staged neatly; no clutter skyline

## Clothing & appearance

Follow the [Wardrobe Guidelines](../08-culture-care/wardrobe-guidelines.md). Default: neat, non-distracting, camera-aware.

## Microphones

- Assume always hot until proven otherwise
- Never vent about people, politics, or inside jokes on a live mic
- Hand off mics intentionally during spoken moments

## Between songs

- Follow the plan for prayer, scripture, or silence
- Tuning happens under cover of pads/prayer when possible
- If a train wreck happens, reset with peace — the room takes its cue from you

## Green room rule

Offstage still represents the team: the green room, the lobby, the parking lot, and the group chat. No grumbling about the congregation, the pastor, or a teammate where others can hear it; take it to the person or a lead.

## Alcohol / substances

Team members serving that day follow church policy; impaired serving is an immediate stop.
