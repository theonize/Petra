# Service Run-of-Show

**Owner:** Director  

## Spine (customize)

1. Building open / tech power path  
2. Team **present** → **ready for run-through**  
3. Line check + pre-service rehearsal  
4. **Freeze** (default: doors) — experiments stop; Worship Lead or Tech Lead calls it once on talkback  
5. Team prayer  
6. Doors / pre-service atmosphere  
7. Service start (countdown or live open)  
8. Worship set / liturgical pieces per plan  
9. Transitions to word, ordinances, response  
10. Closing song / benediction support as planned  
11. Post-service play-out (optional)  
12. Strike + debrief notes  

Clocks for each step live in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md); the week’s service plan names the **Platform lead**.

## Owners during service

| Domain | Primary | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| Musical calls (entries, holds, endings, cut/extend) | Worship Lead as **Platform lead** | Director |
| House mix | FOH | Tech Lead |
| Lyrics | Media | Stream op if cross-trained |
| Lights | Lighting | Tech Lead |
| Pastoral deviations | Platform lead listens to the pastor | — |
| Stopping the service for a person (medical, safety) | Any lead who sees it | — |

## Quiet professionalism

- In-ear chatter stays minimal and kind
- Side-stage conversations away from open mics
- If the pastor extends, band watches the Platform lead — don’t invent a concert

When something breaks: [Incidents & Contingencies](incidents-and-contingencies.md).
