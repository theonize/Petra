# Incidents & Contingencies

**Owner:** Director + Tech Lead  

## Principles

1. **People safety first** (electrical, structural, medical, security).  
2. **Preserve the moment** second — quiet fixes over dramatic ones.  
3. **Communicate once** on a known channel; avoid five parallel panic threads.

For danger, medical emergencies, fire, or security threats, call local emergency services when needed and follow the church's emergency plan. Do not improvise beyond your role or training. Production continuity is never more important than a person.

## Common failures

| Failure | Immediate action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Lead vocal mic dies | Swap spare / channel; Worship Lead keeps singing if handheld available | Stage + FOH |
| Click/tracks die | Worship Lead counts band in; drop to live arrangement | Worship Lead + playback owner |
| Lyrics freeze | Holding slide; lead vocal carries; switch to or reboot backup | Media |
| Stream drop | In-room continues; restart encoder when safe | Stream |
| Power blip | Stay calm; follow power-on SOP; pastoral cover | Tech Lead |
| Medical emergency in room | Stop the service if needed; call emergency services; alert the church's designated response lead; clear access and follow trained direction | Platform lead |
| Security threat | Follow church emergency plan — not improvised heroics | All |

## After any significant incident

- Care for people before gear postmortems
- Write a short factual note: what, when, impact, fix, prevention
- Update SOPs if the doc was wrong or missing
