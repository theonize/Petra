## Categories

| Category | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Church-owned shared | Consoles, mics, stands, DIs | Stay on inventory list |
| Church-owned assigned | IEM pack #3 | Still church property |
| Personal | Player’s guitar, pedalboard | Owner maintains; label cables |
| Loaned | Guest amp | Document who/when |

## Personal gear

- Clearly labeled
- Not assumed available next week unless agreed
- Church not automatically liable — follow church policy for damage
