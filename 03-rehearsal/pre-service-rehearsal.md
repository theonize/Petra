# Pre-Service Rehearsal

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** MD + Tech Lead  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Purpose

Confirm readiness and settle nerves — not rebuild the set.

## Defaults

- Shorter than midweek
- Prefer full runs and transitions over microscopic notes
- Protect prayer and pastoral prep time

## Suggested flow

1. Line check / IEMs
2. Problem spots only (max 1–2)
3. Opener + one transition-heavy moment
4. Vocal blend pass if needed
5. Platforms quiet for doors / countdown per church norm
6. Team prayer
