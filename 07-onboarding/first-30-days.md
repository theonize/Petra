# First 30 Days

**Owner:** Section lead / mentor  

## Goals

- Know the weekly clock, where to park and get in, and who to text ([Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md))
- Meet the Worship Lead / Tech Lead and section peers
- Serve at least once in a supported slot
- Read Mission & Vision, Values, your role charter, and your quick-reference handout (save the Freedom in Form study for a team night)
- Get accounts/access to planning tools

## Mentor checklist

- [ ] Welcome + culture conversation
- [ ] Protection policy and designated reporter given
- [ ] Tool access granted
- [ ] Role charter and handout walked through
- [ ] Shadow scheduled
- [ ] First serve scheduled with backup nearby
- [ ] 2-week check-in
- [ ] 30-day feedback both directions; qualified-for-solo sign-off if ready
