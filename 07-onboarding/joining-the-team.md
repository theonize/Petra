# Joining the Team

**Owner:** Director  

## Who we’re looking for

- Growing disciples who call this church home (or are actively becoming so)
- Teachable musicians/techs
- Reliable communicators
- Servants who can handle both spiritual moments and practical grit

## Path

1. **Interest** — conversation with Director / team lead  
2. **Church alignment** — membership/attendance expectations per church policy  
3. **Safety** — background check / child protection steps if required  
4. **Assessment** — musical or tech skills ([Auditions & Assessments](auditions-and-assessments.md))  
5. **Shadow** — watch a real Sunday  
6. **Provisional serve** — low-risk slots with a mentor  
7. **Active roster** — normal scheduling  

## Disqualifiers / pauses (examples)

- Unwillingness to prepare
- Persistent division or platform ego
- Ignoring protection or safety policies
- Life season that needs rest (pause is pastoral, not punishment)
