# Shadow & Training

**Owner:** Tech Lead / Worship Lead  

## Shadow rules

- Shadows are introduced to the team; not anonymous lurkers
- Hands on gear only with operator OK
- Ask questions between songs, not during quiet pastoral moments
- Confidentiality: what you hear in prayer/team talk stays in the team — except where the church’s reporting obligations apply ([Safety & Protection](../08-culture-care/safety-and-protection.md))

## Training progression (tech example)

1. Observe full service  
2. Run checklist with coach  
3. Own a segment (e.g. wireless batteries + labeling)  
4. Own role with coach on coms  
5. **Qualified for solo serve** — shadow steps complete and the Tech Lead signs the onboarding checklist  

## Band progression

1. Rehearsal sit-in  
2. Partial set  
3. Full set with simple arrangements  
4. **Qualified** — Worship Lead signs the onboarding checklist; normal rotation
