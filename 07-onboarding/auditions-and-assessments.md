# Auditions & Assessments

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** MD / Tech Lead  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Heart posture

Assessment is matching gifts to needs — not American Idol. Candidates should leave encouraged even if not placed yet.

## Musicians / vocalists

**Typically evaluate**

- Time and pitch
- Ability to learn a provided chart
- Blend and dynamic control
- Attitude under notes
- Gear readiness (for instrumentalists)

**Suggested flow**

1. Short warm-up  
2. Familiar worship song in published key  
3. Harmony or part-singing sample (vocals)  
4. Click exercise if tracks are common here  
5. Conversation: prep habits, availability, motives  

## Tech

**Typically evaluate**

- Calm under mild pressure
- Signal flow basics for the role
- Following a checklist
- Communication on coms
- Willingness to log issues

Prefer practical shadow tasks over trivia quizzes.
