# Scheduling Policy

**Owner:** Director  

## Purpose

Put the right people on the right services early enough to prepare well — including the rehearsal that belongs to that service.

## Policy (must)

1. Schedules are published ahead of time — normal weekends and special services (Good Friday, Christmas, conferences) each have a lead time set in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md) (typical: 2–4 weeks; 6–8 weeks for specials).
2. A scheduled teammate is responsible for the slot until a **confirmed** swap or release.
3. Only **qualified** people may cover a role — listed for it on the active roster, or signed off by the Worship Lead / Tech Lead (see [Definitions](../00-foundation/definitions.md)).
4. Minors serving on platform follow church protection + guardian rules (see [Safety & Protection](../08-culture-care/safety-and-protection.md)).
5. **Rehearsal is part of the schedule for band and tech.** Accepting a serve slot includes the **prior group rehearsal** for that service. Default: no prior rehearsal → do not serve; exceptions are granted in advance, not assumed (see [Rehearsal Standards](../03-rehearsal/rehearsal-standards.md)).
6. **Media at rehearsal runs house/overhead and confidence** — production path is practiced midweek, not invented Sunday.

## Procedure — building a roster

1. Pull series themes / pastor notes if available.
2. Draft **band + tech** needs from set complexity (tech slots include midweek).
3. Honor stated availability and rest rhythms — availability means rehearsal **and** service unless noted (musicians **and** tech).
4. Avoid burning the same “always yes” people every week.
5. Publish in the official tool; announce notable gaps (including media/FOH holes at rehearsal).
6. When someone cannot make rehearsal, resolve **before Sunday**: release, swap, or simplify.

## Rest & load

- Target sustainable frequency per person (typical baseline 1–2× / month, with exceptions for leaders; the church’s number is in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md)).
- After heavy production weeks, intentionally lighten load.
