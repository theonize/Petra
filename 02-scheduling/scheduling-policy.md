# Scheduling Policy

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12  
**Review cadence:** quarterly

## Purpose

Put the right people on the right services early enough to prepare well.

## Policy (must)

1. Schedules are published at least **[TBD: e.g. 2–4 weeks]** ahead for normal weekends.
2. Special services (Good Friday, Christmas, conferences) are blocked **[TBD: e.g. 6–8 weeks]** ahead when possible.
3. A scheduled teammate is responsible for the slot until a **confirmed** swap or release.
4. Only qualified people may cover a role (Tech Lead / MD / Worship Lead defines “qualified”).
5. Minors serving on platform follow church protection + guardian rules (see culture/care).

## Procedure — building a roster

1. Pull series themes / pastor notes if available.
2. Draft band + tech needs from set complexity.
3. Honor stated availability and rest rhythms.
4. Avoid burning the same “always yes” people every week.
5. Publish in the official tool; announce notable gaps.

## Rest & load

- Target sustainable frequency per person (**TBD: e.g. 1–2x / month** baseline, with exceptions for leaders).
- After heavy production weeks, intentionally lighten load.
