# Licensing (CCLI & Friends)

**Owner:** Director  

## Purpose

Stay legally and ethically clean when reproducing lyrics, printing charts, streaming, and recording. *Operational awareness, not legal advice — coverage varies by country and product; check the current terms of your licence provider.*

## Policy (must)

1. Only use songs we have appropriate rights to reproduce for our contexts.
2. Report usage per our CCLI (or other) license process — a named owner, on a cadence.
3. Streaming/recording may require additional coverage — follow church policy.
4. Do not paste full copyrighted lyrics into public GitHub pages if that would exceed rights; keep decks in licensed tools.

## The buckets (fill in Our Congregation)

“We have CCLI” is not one thing. Record, in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md), which of these the church actually holds and who owns each:

| Use | Typically needs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Projecting / printing lyrics for the congregation | Reproduction licence | The basic one |
| Charts / lead sheets for the team | Chart/SongSelect-type licence or a legal chart source | Not covered by reproduction alone |
| Rehearsal audio shared with the team | Rehearsal/recording provision or legal source | Sharing a ripped track in the chat is not covered |
| Streaming or posting the service online | Streaming licence | Covers congregational singing of licensed songs on the stream |
| Playing master recordings / multitracks **on the stream** | Separate permission | **Playing tracks in the room is not the same as broadcasting masters** |
| Walk-in / secular recordings | Separate rights question | Outside the worship-set policy |
| Originals | Writer splits documented before wide use | |

## Practical

- Prefer Planning Center / SongSelect / licensed sources for charts
- Before a new title enters Trial, confirm it is in the licensed catalog (new-song gate)
- Track license numbers and the reporting owner in Our Congregation; private details stay out of the public repo
- Point to your provider’s current summary rather than restating coverage here
