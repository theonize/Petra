# Charts, Keys & Tempos

**Owner:** Worship Lead  

## Source of truth

The plan’s attached chart + stated key/tempo beat any random YouTube memory.

## When

Keys, forms, and charts are published by the **keys/charts deadline** — default at least 48 hours before ready-to-rehearse; the church’s actual clock lives in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md). Players acknowledge a chart or key change within 12 hours or before the next rehearsal, whichever is sooner. Rehearsal may still amend; amendments are called out.

## Chart standards

- Section labels match what the Worship Lead calls: `I` intro · `V1` verse 1 · `PC` pre-chorus · `C` chorus · `B` bridge · `T` tag · `E` ending
- Repeats and tags explicit
- Capo notation clear for guitars
- Lyric sheet synced for media

## Keys

- Choose for congregation first, leader second, ego never — can an untrained voice on row 12 sing it?
- Publish the key by the deadline so vocalists can flag trouble
- If capoing creates chaos, prefer a real key change chart

## Tempos

- BPM published when click/tracks used
- Feel notes welcome (“straight”, “half-time chorus”, “push”)

## Storage

Canonical library location: see [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md).
