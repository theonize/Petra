# Song Lifecycle

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead + MD  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Stages

1. **Scout** — heard/suggested; theological & congregational fit review  
2. **Trial** — introduced with clear teach moments  
3. **Core** — known by the room; appears in regular rotation  
4. **Rest** — intentionally shelved to keep the diet fresh  
5. **Archive** — retained for special use, not active weekly  

## Intake criteria (discussion starters)

- Lyric truthfulness and clarity
- Singability for *this* congregation
- Band/tech complexity vs available volunteers
- Overlap with songs already in rotation
- Pastoral season fit

## Introducing a new song

- Teach strategy agreed (call-and-response, melody first, etc.)
- Charts + lyrics deck ready before first full-band week
- Evaluate after 2–3 outings: keep, rework, or rest
