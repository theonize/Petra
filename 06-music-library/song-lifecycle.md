# Song Lifecycle

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Song Selector + Worship Lead + MD  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-13

## Stages

1. **Scout** — heard/suggested; theological & congregational fit review (**Song Selector**)  
2. **Trial** — introduced with clear teach moments  
3. **Core** — known by the room; appears in regular rotation  
4. **Rest** — intentionally shelved to keep the diet fresh  
5. **Archive** — retained for special use, not active weekly  

## Role split

| Stage work | Primary |
|---|---|
| What enters the diet / which titles this week | **Song Selector** (Worship Lead covers) |
| Can we arrange and rehearse it well | **MD** |
| Can media/tech support it | Media / Tech Lead |
| Keep / rework / rest after outings | Song Selector + MD + Worship Lead |

## Intake criteria (discussion starters)

- Lyric truthfulness and clarity
- Singability for *this* congregation
- Band/tech complexity vs available volunteers
- Overlap with songs already in rotation
- Pastoral season fit

## Introducing a new song

- Teach strategy agreed (call-and-response, melody first, etc.) — Song Selector + MD
- Charts + lyrics deck ready before first full-band week — MD + Media
- Evaluate after 2–3 outings: keep, rework, or rest
