# Mission & Vision

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead / Pastoral oversight  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12  
**Review cadence:** annual

## Mission

Help the church encounter God through music, word, and well-run technical craft — so the room can sing, pray, and respond without distraction.

## Vision

A team that is:

- **Spiritually grounded** — servants first, musicians second
- **Musically prepared** — charts learned, parts clear, dynamics intentional
- **Technically invisible** — sound, lights, and media serve the moment
- **Relationally safe** — people can grow, fail forward, and stay

## What success looks like (signals, not scoreboard)

- The congregation sings
- Transitions feel calm
- Teammates know expectations before Sunday
- New volunteers can be onboarded without tribal knowledge
- Gear and people are cared for after the last song

## Non-goals

- Platform culture or celebrity musicianship
- Complexity that only the same three people can run
- Policies that exist to control rather than free the team
