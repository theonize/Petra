# Definitions

**Owner:** Director  

Language shapes culture. We use plain operational words without turning Sunday into a show.

**North star:** musical leadership exists to **encourage the congregation to sing**. Petra exists to hold mundane structure so artists are free to do that well — see [Mission & Vision](mission-vision.md) and [Scope & Authority](scope-and-authority.md).

## Framing: worship, serve, perform

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Worship** | The aim of the gathering: the church responding to God (song, prayer, word, sacrament, silence). Everything the team does is in service of **corporate worship**, not a concert. |
| **Congregational singing** | Primary success signal for the worship set — the room’s voice, not only the platform’s. |
| **Worship set / musical worship** | The planned songs and related moments the team leads so the **congregation** can sing and respond. |
| **Serve / serving** | Preferred operational language for being on the roster that weekend (“Who is serving Sunday?”). Emphasizes ministry and team responsibility. |
| **On platform / scheduled** | You are assigned a role for a specific service (or rehearsal). “Platform” means visible or audible leadership space — stage, mics, consoles in the room’s workflow — not celebrity. |
| **Perform / performance** | **Casual use only:** “the Sunday you perform” = the service you are scheduled to serve. **Not our theology of the room:** we do not “perform at” the congregation. If a doc needs a neutral verb for “execute the musical/technical plan,” prefer **serve**, **lead**, or **run**. |
| **Player / band player** | Instrumentalist scheduled on the band (guitar, bass, keys, drums, etc.). |
| **Teammate** | Anyone on the worship ministry roster — band, vocals, or tech. |

## Roles & production

Use these names everywhere — charters, plans, chat. (Older notes may use other names for the same seats; the [Changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) records the history.)

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Pastor / pastoral oversight** | The pastor or the pastor’s named delegate. Final say; appoints the Director; owns doctrine. |
| **Director** | Owns the congregation’s musical worship under the direction of the pastor; appoints and develops the team leads (Worship Lead, Song Selector, Tech Lead); accountable to the pastor for the worship of the gathered congregation and the health of the team |
| **Worship Lead** | Leads the music: keys, forms, charts, rehearsal, musical calls during the service |
| **Song Selector** | Chooses songs / draft setlists for congregational singing and pastoral fit; hands off to Worship Lead for musical execution |
| **Tech Lead** | Owns FOH/monitor/lights/media standards and tech roster health |
| **Platform lead** *(hat)* | The person scheduled to address the congregation and whom band and media watch during the set — cues entries, holds, endings; speaks or prays between songs; listens to the pastor for deviations. Usually the Worship Lead; may be the Director or a vocalist. Named on the service plan. |
| **Service Planner** *(hat)* | Builds the order of service / liturgy flow beyond the musical set; does not pick titles or keys. Usually the Director or Worship Lead. |
| **Section lead** *(hat)* | Experienced player or tech who coaches a slice of the team (vocals, rhythm, media …); appointed by the Worship Lead or Tech Lead; first stop for questions and the first step of escalation. If none is named, the Worship Lead / Tech Lead is your section lead. |
| **FOH** | Front of house — the audio mix the congregation hears (and the engineer who runs it) |
| **Monitors / IEM** | What musicians hear on stage (wedges or in-ear monitors) |
| **Media** | Lyrics, graphics, countdown, lower-thirds, **confidence monitors** (aka video monitors / stage video) |
| **Stream / Broadcast** | Online mix and video (if applicable) |
| **Section** | Vocals, rhythm, keys, guitars, drums, tech, etc. |

## Time & readiness

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Present** | In the building |
| **Ready** (ready to rehearse / ready for run-through) | At your position, personally prepared, patched, tuned, able to begin. **Call time** is another name for this — it means ready to work, not walking in |
| **Service-ready** | Pre-service checks complete; clothing, equipment, water, and position settled |
| **Freeze** | The moment experiments stop: no new patches, arrangements, or console changes unless safety or failure. Default: doors (or T−10); called on talkback by the Worship Lead or Tech Lead. *Monitor freeze* — no major mix changes after the final run — is separate |
| **Downbeat** | First musical moment of rehearsal or set |
| **Practice (personal)** | Individual preparation at home — learning parts before group time |
| **Rehearsal (group)** | Scheduled ensemble time (typically midweek) for the upcoming service(s) |
| **Prior rehearsal** | The main group rehearsal tied to the service you are scheduled to serve (see rehearsal attendance policy) |
| **Pre-service rehearsal** | Short Sunday/same-day run-through; confirms readiness — does **not** replace midweek rehearsal |
| **Qualified** | Listed for that role on the active roster, or signed off for it by the domain lead (Worship Lead / Tech Lead). The prior-rehearsal rule still applies |

## Music & staging

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Chart** | Authoritative roadmap (chord chart, lead sheet, or full chart). Section tags: `V1` verse · `PC` pre-chorus · `C` chorus · `B` bridge · `T` tag · `E` ending / `I` intro |
| **Pad / tracks** | Backing textures or click/guide used with or without live players |
| **Click** | Metronome in the in-ears that keeps band and tracks together; the congregation never hears it |
| **House / overhead lyrics** | Words projected for the **congregation** (and usually stream) |
| **Confidence monitors** | Preferred term for the screens musicians and platform leaders face — lyrics, cues, and/or charts so eyes stay up. **Also called:** video monitors, stage video, confidence screens. |
| **Music stand** | Physical stand for paper/tablet charts — **exception path**, not the default (see stage display standards) |
| **Swap** | Trading a scheduled slot with another qualified teammate |
| **Green room rule** | Offstage conduct still represents the team — see [Platform Etiquette](../04-service/platform-etiquette.md) |

Church-specific names, clocks, rooms, and tools live in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md).
