# Values

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12  
**Review cadence:** annual

These values referee gray areas when a checklist does not.

## 1. Presence over polish

We prepare hard so we can be present. Excellence is a form of love for the room — not a substitute for dependence on God.

## 2. Clarity is kindness

Ambiguous expectations create anxiety. Publish the plan, the key, the call time, and the owner.

## 3. Team over talent

We would rather have a faithful, prepared team player than a gifted lone ranger. Parts serve the arrangement; arrangements serve the congregation.

## 4. Stewardship

Time, gear, budget, and attention are limited. We leave rooms better than we found them and flag problems early.

## 5. Feedback in love

Notes after rehearsal are normal. Public shaming is not. Correct privately when possible; celebrate publicly often.

## 6. Sunday starts midweek

The service is the fruit of planning, personal practice, and communication — not a wing-and-a-prayer meetup.

## 7. Safety and honor

We honor the church’s protection policies, stage boundaries, and one-another care. No ministry moment is worth harming a person.
