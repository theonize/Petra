# Scope & Authority

**Owner:** Director + Pastoral oversight  

## Why Petra exists

Petra is a **framework for the mundane**.

Call times, swaps, input lists, lyric routing, rehearsal attendance, gear care — the repeatable stuff — lives here on purpose. When those decisions are already made, **artists and tech are free** to focus on leading worship and helping the congregation sing.

Petra is not a box, it is a skeleton.
If a page starts micromanaging artistry instead of clearing the runway, rewrite the page.

## What Petra governs

- Worship team culture and expectations
- Musician / vocalist / tech operating procedures
- Scheduling norms and swap rules
- Rehearsal and service run-of-show standards
- Music library, licensing awareness, chart conventions
- Equipment care routines owned by the worship/tech ministry
- Onboarding paths for band and tech volunteers
- Shared language (so we stop re-arguing definitions every week)

## What Petra does not govern

- Full church employee handbook / HR
- Facility master calendar owned by other ministries
- Doctrinal statements (owned by elders/pastors)
- Child protection policy master text (we **comply** and point to it)
- Budget approval authority (we follow church finance process)
- **Artistic calling in the moment** — dynamics, prayer, spontaneous response, Spirit-led restraint or boldness within the plan and pastoral covering

## Design rule for every doc

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Clear defaults and owners | Endless preference debates at downbeat |
| Checklists for repeatable risk | Scripts that kill presence |
| “Must” only for safety, care, or congregational clarity | Rules that exist to flex control |
| Exceptions with a named decider | Tribal knowledge |

## Decision rights

Line of authority: **Pastor → Director → team leads** (Worship Lead, Song Selector, Tech Lead). The pastor has final say; responsibility and accountability for the congregation’s worship and the team’s health rest with the Director.

Who **proposes**, who **decides**, who is **told**:

| Decision | Propose | Decide | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appoint the Director | Pastor | Pastor | Team |
| Songs / setlist titles for a service | **Song Selector** publishes the proposed set by the weekly deadline | **Director confirms** — the set is *fixed* once confirmed, or by the confirm-by time if the Director is silent (see [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md)). After that only a named exception changes titles | Worship Lead, Service Planner, Media, band |
| Keys, tempos, forms for a service | Worship Lead | Worship Lead (Director if sensitive); published by the keys/charts deadline | Band, Media, Song Selector |
| Arrange / cut a song section | Worship Lead | Worship Lead (Director if sensitive) | Band |
| Change call times permanently | Director | Director + pastor (or named delegate) | Full team |
| Appoint Worship Lead, Song Selector, or Tech Lead | Director | Director (pastor consulted) | Team |
| Major gear purchase | Tech Lead | Church finance / pastor process | Director |
| Exception to a policy for one week | Role owner | Director | Affected teammates |
| Exception to a song-selection avoidance (e.g. a Scripture chorus sung in God’s voice) | Song Selector / Director | Director **with pastoral oversight**, recorded on the setlist worksheet | Team |
| Edit Petra policy | Anyone | Director (or named delegate) | Team via changelog / announce |

## Escalation path

1. Peer / section lead  
2. Worship Lead or Tech Lead (domain)  
3. Director  
4. Pastoral oversight  

Pastoral-care and safety issues skip the chain and go straight to appropriate pastoral / safety contacts.
