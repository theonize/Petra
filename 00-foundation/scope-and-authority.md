# Scope & Authority

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead + Pastoral oversight  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12  
**Review cadence:** annual

## What Petra governs

- Worship team culture and expectations
- Musician / vocalist / tech operating procedures
- Scheduling norms and swap rules
- Rehearsal and service run-of-show standards
- Music library, licensing awareness, chart conventions
- Equipment care routines owned by the worship/tech ministry
- Onboarding paths for band and tech volunteers

## What Petra does not govern (unless linked)

- Full church employee handbook / HR
- Facility master calendar owned by other ministries
- Doctrinal statements (owned by elders/pastors)
- Child protection policy master text (we **comply** and point to it)
- Budget approval authority (we follow church finance process)

## Decision rights (RACI-lite)

| Decision | Propose | Decide | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setlist & keys for a service | MD / Service planner | Worship Lead | Band, tech |
| Arrange / cut a song section | MD | MD (Worship Lead if sensitive) | Band |
| Change call times permanently | Worship Lead | Worship Lead + Pastor liaison | Full team |
| Hire/appoint MD or Tech Lead | Worship Lead | Pastoral oversight | Team |
| Major gear purchase | Tech Lead | Church finance / pastor process | Worship Lead |
| Exception to an `active` policy for one week | Role owner | Worship Lead | Affected teammates |
| Edit Petra `active` policy | Anyone | Worship Lead (or designee) | Team via changelog / announce |

## Escalation path

1. Peer / section lead  
2. Music Director (MD) or Tech Lead (domain)  
3. Worship Lead  
4. Pastoral oversight  

Pastoral-care and safety issues skip the chain and go straight to appropriate pastoral / safety contacts.
