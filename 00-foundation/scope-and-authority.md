# Scope & Authority

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead + Pastoral oversight  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-13  
**Review cadence:** annual

## Why Petra exists

Petra is a **framework for the mundane**.

Call times, swaps, input lists, lyric routing, rehearsal attendance, gear care — the repeatable stuff — lives here on purpose. When those decisions are already made, **artists and tech are free** to focus on leading worship and helping the congregation sing.

Petra is not a creative straitjacket. If a page starts micromanaging artistry instead of clearing the runway, rewrite the page.

## What Petra governs

- Worship team culture and expectations
- Musician / vocalist / tech operating procedures
- Scheduling norms and swap rules
- Rehearsal and service run-of-show standards
- Music library, licensing awareness, chart conventions
- Equipment care routines owned by the worship/tech ministry
- Onboarding paths for band and tech volunteers
- Shared language (so we stop re-arguing definitions every week)

## What Petra does not govern (unless linked)

- Full church employee handbook / HR
- Facility master calendar owned by other ministries
- Doctrinal statements (owned by elders/pastors)
- Child protection policy master text (we **comply** and point to it)
- Budget approval authority (we follow church finance process)
- **Artistic calling in the moment** — dynamics, prayer, spontaneous response, Spirit-led restraint or boldness within the plan and pastoral covering

## Design rule for every doc

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Clear defaults and owners | Endless preference debates at downbeat |
| Checklists for repeatable risk | Scripts that kill presence |
| “Must” only for safety, care, or congregational clarity | Rules that exist to flex control |
| Exceptions with a named decider | Tribal knowledge |

## Decision rights (RACI-lite)

| Decision | Propose | Decide | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Song menu / setlist titles for a service | **Song Selector** | Worship Lead | MD, Service Planner, Media, band |
| Keys, tempos, forms for a service | MD | MD (Worship Lead if sensitive) | Band, Media, Song Selector |
| Arrange / cut a song section | MD | MD (Worship Lead if sensitive) | Band |
| Change call times permanently | Worship Lead | Worship Lead + Pastor liaison | Full team |
| Hire/appoint MD, Song Selector, or Tech Lead | Worship Lead | Pastoral oversight | Team |
| Major gear purchase | Tech Lead | Church finance / pastor process | Worship Lead |
| Exception to an `active` policy for one week | Role owner | Worship Lead | Affected teammates |
| Edit Petra `active` policy | Anyone | Worship Lead (or designee) | Team via changelog / announce |

## Escalation path

1. Peer / section lead  
2. Music Director (MD) or Tech Lead (domain)  
3. Worship Lead  
4. Pastoral oversight  

Pastoral-care and safety issues skip the chain and go straight to appropriate pastoral / safety contacts.
