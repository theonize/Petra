# Wardrobe Guidelines

**Owner:** Director  

## Goal

Clothes that let you move, kneel, and play; that read well on camera; and that keep the congregation’s attention on the lyric, not the platform.

## Defaults (edit in Our Congregation)

- Clean, intentional, comfortable for movement — jeans and a plain shirt are fine; so is a dress you can kneel in
- Shoes you can stand and move in safely
- Nothing that talks over the lyric: large logos, slogans, political or other-church merch, jewelry that hits the mic
- Stage light is harsher than bathroom light — check sheer fabrics and hemlines with a teammate you choose
- Team colors, if leadership sets them: [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md)

## If something needs saying

The Director or Worship Lead says it **privately**, before service when possible. Nobody is corrected from the stage or in the group chat.

## Special services

Christmas, baptisms, funerals, conferences may carry specific dress notes — on the service plan (“Special dress”).
