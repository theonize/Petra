# Wardrobe Guidelines

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Director  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Goal

Non-distracting, camera-aware, team-cohesive appearance that fits our church culture.

## Defaults (edit freely)

- Clean, intentional, comfortable for movement
- Avoid large political/controversial graphics
- Mind necklines, length, and sheer fabrics under stage lighting
- Shoes you can stand and move in safely
- Team colors / “no neon chaos” rules if leadership sets them

## Special services

Christmas, baptisms, funerals, conferences may have specific dress notes on the plan.
