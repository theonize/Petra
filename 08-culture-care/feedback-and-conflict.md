# Feedback & Conflict

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Director  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Musical notes

- Expected in rehearsal
- Specific and actionable
- About the part, not the person’s worth
- Big corrections preferably not shouted across the stage if a quiet word works

## Relational conflict

1. Go to the person directly when safe  
2. If unresolved, bring a lead  
3. If still unresolved, Director / pastoral oversight  
4. No faction-building in group chat  

## When feedback is hard to receive

Take a breath. Clarify. Try the note. Debrief later if needed.

## When feedback is poorly given

Leads coach the giver — standards apply to leaders too.
