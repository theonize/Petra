# Team Culture

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Worship Lead  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## We are a ministry team

Skill matters. Character and reliability matter more over time.

## Norms

- We prepare so others can relax
- We celebrate catches and covers
- We don’t weaponize spiritual language to avoid practical responsibility
- We make newcomers less weird, not more
- We protect unity with pastors and the wider church

## Humor

Welcome. Punching down is not. Inside jokes that exclude new people get retired.
