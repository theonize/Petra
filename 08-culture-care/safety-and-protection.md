# Safety & Protection

**Owner:** Director  

## Rule

**Adhere to your congregation’s protection / safeguarding policy.** It wins over any worship preference, and this page does not replace it. What follows is the worship-ministry baseline; where your church’s policy is stricter, the church’s policy applies.

## Before you serve

- Every teammate receives the church’s protection policy and the name/number of the **designated reporter** (kept in [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md), not in this skeleton)
- Background check and protection training as the church requires — **blocking if minors serve**
- If those blanks are empty, this church has not finished adopting Petra; ask the Director before serving

## Baseline (unless church policy is stricter)

- Minors on team: ratios, check-in, and guardian rules per church policy
- **Never alone in secluded spaces with a minor**; two adults for closed-room training
- No private messaging with minors — copy a parent or a lead; no unsupervised rides
- IEM fitting, wardrobe help, and one-to-one coaching happen in open space
- Photos / stream of minors follow the church’s release policy

## If you have a concern

- Report to the designated reporter / church channel **promptly**; if someone is in immediate danger, call emergency services first
- Do not investigate, do not confront the person, do not promise a child secrecy
- Nothing about it goes in the band chat, the incident log, or this repo

## Physical safety

- Cable ramps / tape where needed; no unsafe DIY rigging; volunteers do not climb, fly, or rewire
- Know exits, first aid, and the AED ([Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md)); use emergency equipment as its prompts and your training direct
- Hearing is people-safety: if anyone reports pain, turn down first ([Tech Overview](../05-tech/tech-overview.md))
- Stop the show for real danger — any lead may call it ([Incidents & Contingencies](../04-service/incidents-and-contingencies.md))
