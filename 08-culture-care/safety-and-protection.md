# Safety & Protection

**Owner:** Director  

## Rule

Church protection / safety policy wins over any worship preference.

This page does not replace the church's safeguarding or emergency plans. Leaders must link those plans, name the reporting route, and brief the team before this runbook is put into use. In an emergency, contact local emergency services and follow the facility plan; do not exceed your training.

## Platform-specific

- Minors on team: ratios, check-in, and guardian rules per church policy
- Never alone in secluded spaces with a minor in ways policy forbids
- Two-adult norms for closed-room training when required
- Report concerns promptly through the church's official safeguarding channel. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## Physical safety

- Cable ramps / tape where needed
- No unsafe DIY rigging
- Know the locations of exits, first-aid supplies, and the AED; use emergency equipment only as directed by its prompts and your training
- Stop the show for real danger
