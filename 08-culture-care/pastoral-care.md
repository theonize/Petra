# Pastoral Care

**Owner:** Director  

## Serving while human

Teams carry grief, burnout, marriage stress, and doubt. We would rather know and adjust the roster than watch someone quietly drown.

## Invitations

- Tell a lead if you need a season off
- You are not disloyal for resting
- Leaders will not always fix everything — but you won’t be alone

## When it is bigger than the band

| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| Danger to self or others; any abuse disclosure | Designated reporter / pastor **the same day** — not the group chat. See [Safety & Protection](safety-and-protection.md) |
| Burnout, grief, a hard season | Section lead or Director; the roster bends |
| Conflict that is not safe to take to the person (power, harassment, a lead is the problem) | Skip the chain: Director or pastor directly |
| Ordinary friction | [Feedback & Conflict](feedback-and-conflict.md) |

Pastoral-care contact: [Our Congregation](../10-templates/our-congregation.md).

## Boundaries

- The worship team is not a counseling center; we route to pastors / care team
- Don’t process someone else’s private story in the band room; what you hear stays in the team — except where the church’s reporting obligations apply
- Dating inside the team is not banned by Petra; if it affects scheduling or power, the Director handles it privately
