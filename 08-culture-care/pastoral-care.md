# Pastoral Care

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Director  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-12

## Serving while human

Teams carry grief, burnout, marriage stress, and doubt. We would rather know and adjust the roster than watch someone quietly drown.

## Invitations

- Tell a lead if you need a season off
- You are not disloyal for resting
- Leaders will not always fix everything — but you won’t be alone

## Boundaries

- Petra is not the counseling center; we route to pastors/care team
- Don’t process someone else’s private story in the band room
- Romantic entanglement patterns that harm team health will be addressed pastorally
